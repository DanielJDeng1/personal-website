Triple Pendulum RL

Repository

A custom MuJoCo/Gymnasium environment for balancing a triple inverted pendulum on a cart. Gymnasium provides a double inverted pendulum environment but not a triple one, so the environment, observation and reward design, and termination conditions were built from scratch. The system dynamics are linearized to compute an LQR baseline, and PPO learns a residual correction on top of it rather than the full control policy.

  • PyTorch
  • MuJoCo
  • Gymnasium
  • PPO / LQR
